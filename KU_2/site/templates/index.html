Dual Priority Application Targets

Unlike tools that pigeonhole candidates into a single tier, this agent explicitly tracks two parallel career tracks for Batch 2027 graduates:

  • Track A — Internships & Apprenticeships: 3 to 6-month engineering internships with stipends, offering immediate industry immersion.
  • Track B — Full-Time Fresher Roles: Graduate Engineer Trainee (GET), Associate Software Engineer, and Junior Backend roles requiring 0 to 1 years experience.

Both categories are evaluated simultaneously and presented as primary "Apply Now" sections in every digest.

Ethical Boundaries & Zero Scraping Charter

Built in strict compliance with platform terms and ethical standards. The agent strictly queries authorized public endpoints and SSL IMAP forwarded alerts. It never performs HTML scraping of LinkedIn, Naukri, or Indeed.
