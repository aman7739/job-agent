System Architecture & Processing Pipeline

An end-to-end overview of how the Job Digest Agent collects, normalizes, evaluates, and delivers verified tech opportunities twice daily.

1

Multi-Source Ingestion with Fault Isolation

The runner executes concurrent asynchronous requests across 8 source adapters. Each source runs within an isolated fault boundary. If a single ATS API experiences downtime or network timeout, the failure is recorded to source_runs while all other sources continue normally without crashing the pipeline.

2

Text Normalization, Location Cleaning & Anti-Traps

Raw HTML postings are stripped of tracking tokens (UTMs, ref codes) and HTML tags. Locations are mapped to canonical metros (e.g., Gurgaon → Gurugram, Calcutta → Kolkata). Anti-trap regex extractors ensure single-letter languages like C and Go are not falsely matched inside ordinary words like CSS or Google.

3

Deduplication & 48-Hour Freshness Cutoff

Company legal suffixes (Inc., Pvt. Ltd., Corporation) are stripped to create canonical company names. A deterministic SHA-256 fingerprint is computed from sha256(company | title | location). Postings older than 48 hours or previously notified fingerprints are dropped to prevent duplicate alerts.

4

Hard Filters & 10-Point Scoring Algorithm

Every fresh job passes through rigorous filters: senior, staff, lead, and manager roles are excluded; required experience cannot exceed 2 years; and named batch years must match 2027. Surviving postings are scored on a 10-point scale:

Category Max Points Scoring Criteria
Skills Overlap 4.0 pts Matched against candidate tech stack (Python, FastAPI, SQL, React, etc.). 4+ skills = 4.0 pts.
Role Match 3.0 pts Exact or semantic match against target engineering roles.
Experience Level 2.0 pts Internships and 0-year fresher roles receive full 2.0 pts.
Location Match 1.0 pt Preferred cities (Delhi NCR, Bengaluru, Mumbai, Pune, Kolkata, Remote) receive 1.0 pt.
5

Dual-Channel Delivery & Fast-Track Urgency Alerts

Ranked jobs meeting the threshold are formatted into dual "Apply Now" sections (Internships/Apprenticeships and Full-Time Roles). Digests are dispatched twice daily at 08:00 AM & 07:00 PM IST via Telegram and SMTP Email. Jobs with deadlines closing in ≤ 4 hours bypass batching and dispatch an immediate high-priority alert.
