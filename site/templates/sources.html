Permitted Sources & Compliance Specifications

The Job Digest Agent operates exclusively via authorized public endpoints and personal alert subscriptions.

Ethical Anti-Scraping Charter

Many career tools violate terms of service by deploying headless browsers, simulating user clicks, or scraping LinkedIn, Naukri, and Indeed behind authentication. The Job Digest Agent rejects these practices. It maintains a strict compliance charter:

  • Zero Headless Browsers: No Selenium, Playwright, or Puppeteer automation.
  • No Platform Scraping: LinkedIn, Naukri, and Indeed are never directly crawled or scraped.
  • Dual-Mailbox Separation: Job board alerts are routed to a dedicated alerts mailbox (Mailbox A) and read via standard SSL IMAP.
  • Rate Limiting Adherence: Every source operates under respectful polling intervals and caching limits.
